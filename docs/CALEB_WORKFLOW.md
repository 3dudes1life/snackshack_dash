# Caleb Daily Workflow

## Morning

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Check **Today Needs Attention**.
3. Confirm new Etsy + Square orders.
4. Check low-stock ingredients.
5. Print today’s production view.

## Before Baking

1. Move orders from `New` to `Ingredients Checked`.
2. Batch similar products together.
3. Confirm pickup/shipping times.

## During Production

Use statuses:

- New
- Ingredients Checked
- Baking
- Cooling
- Packaged
- Ready
- Shipped
- Picked Up
- Complete

## End of Day

1. Mark completed orders.
2. Review profit.
3. Check tomorrow's queue.
4. Update ingredient stock.
5. Note customer preferences.

## Dashboard Rule

If Caleb opens one screen, he should know:

- What needs baking
- What is due today
- What orders are late or risky
- What ingredients are low
- How much profit is in the queue
